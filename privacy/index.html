1. Who is responsible for your data?

For event or service fulfilment, an independent organiser or provider may also act as a separate controller. ODPC registration: [ADD ODPC REGISTRATION]. Privacy contact: hitkenyaevents@gmail.com; data protection contact or officer, if applicable: [DATA PROTECTION CONTACT].

This notice is intended to reflect the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. It covers current website interactions and describes additional account, payment and check-in processing only where those features are activated.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email, phone, role, organisation and registration enquiries.
  • Listings and requests: event, vendor, venue, tour, vehicle or accommodation details and messages you submit.
  • Transactions: where integrated, order details, amount, payment method and transaction references. Full card credentials and M-Pesa PINs should be entered only in an authorised provider interface, never in our enquiry forms.
  • Event entry: where enabled, ticket identifiers, QR validation results and check-in times.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device information, security logs and, with analytics consent, usage and visit information.
  • Local browser data: saved marketplace listings, reviews you enter on this device, display theme and cookie preferences. Local reviews are not automatically published to other visitors.

Some current registration screens include identity-document fields. Do not submit ID copies or face-verification photographs until a secure verification process and dedicated notice are provided. If introduced, verification must have a defined lawful basis, limited access, appropriate safeguards and a documented retention period [VERIFICATION PROCESS]. We do not claim biometric matching is currently available.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

We process necessary information to respond to requests, manage contractual or pre-contractual steps, facilitate ticketing and listings, provide support and fulfil transactions. We may rely on legal obligations for accounting, tax and lawful regulatory requests; on legitimate interests for proportionate fraud prevention, security and service improvement where these do not override your rights; and on consent for optional analytics and marketing where required. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting lawful processing before withdrawal. We will not repurpose personal data incompatibly without a lawful basis and appropriate notice.

4. Who receives information

We may share necessary information with the relevant organiser or provider for fulfilment, approved payment processors for transactions, hosting or technical service providers, and Google Analytics only after analytics consent on the homepage. WhatsApp enquiries are sent by you through WhatsApp when you press Send; its own privacy terms apply. We may disclose information to competent authorities when legally required or to protect lawful rights. Sharing must be proportionate to its purpose. [CONFIRM PROCESSOR LIST AND CONTRACTUAL SAFEGUARDS]. We do not authorise recipients to use attendee information for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as necessary for its purpose, legal obligations, security and legitimate dispute resolution. Confirm the operational retention schedule before enabling server-side collection: [ACCOUNT RETENTION], [TRANSACTION RETENTION], [CHECK-IN RETENTION], [SUPPORT RETENTION] and [VERIFICATION RETENTION]. The consent choice is retained in browser storage for 180 days before renewal; local reviews, saved listings and display settings persist until removed or cleared by the user. You can clear local data through browser settings.

6. Security and incidents

We aim to apply proportionate technical and organisational safeguards including restricted access, secure transmission, data minimisation and appropriate processor controls. No online service is completely risk-free. Where a qualifying breach occurs, we will notify the ODPC and affected persons within the time and circumstances required by law. The current static website does not provide secure server-side identity verification or persistent account infrastructure. Do not upload sensitive documents to an unapproved channel.

7. Your rights

Under Kenyan data protection law, you may be informed about processing, request access to your information, request correction of inaccuracies, object to processing, and request deletion of false or misleading data or erasure where applicable. You may also request restriction and portability where the law provides, withdraw consent, and exercise safeguards concerning solely automated decisions with significant effects. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

Send a request to hitkenyaevents@gmail.com or use our contact link. We may verify your identity proportionately before disclosing information. We will respond within the applicable statutory timeframe. You may also clear device-local information yourself through browser settings.

8. Children's information

Independent registration is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Where a child participates in a family event or service, processing of a child's personal data must satisfy applicable parental or guardian consent requirements and protect the child's best interests. If you believe a child has submitted information without appropriate authorisation, contact us so we can assess and take appropriate action.

9. International transfers

Some infrastructure or third-party services may process information outside Kenya. Where such transfers occur, we must establish a valid legal basis and the safeguards required by Kenyan law. Sensitive data transfers require the applicable additional protections. [CONFIRM HOSTING LOCATIONS, RECIPIENTS AND TRANSFER SAFEGUARDS].

10. Cookies, external services and communications

See the Cookie Policy for browser storage and analytics controls. We use optional analytics only with consent on pages that load the integration. External photos, links, Google Fonts and communication platforms may involve separate third-party requests or processing; they are not controlled by the analytics preference. Marketing communications must follow applicable consent and opt-out requirements. You may unsubscribe or contact us to stop marketing.

11. Complaints and updates

You may contact our privacy team or make a complaint directly to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) using its official complaint form. We will not require you to waive this right. We will update this policy when our practices change and provide appropriate notice. See the ODPC guidance on data subject rights for more information.
