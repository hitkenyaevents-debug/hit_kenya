People, ideas and experiences

Kenya has never lacked energy. From the people behind the stage to the friends in the crowd, every experience starts with someone who makes it happen. HIT KENYA exists to bring those people, ideas and opportunities together.

01

Where it begins.

Every memorable event begins with an idea and a person willing to make it real. HIT KENYA is built around the connection between those people and the audiences they bring together.

02

Get the essentials right.

Clear event information, ticketing, promotion and the right support matter. Our platform brings these pieces closer together so organisers and audiences can focus on the experience.

03

Make room for more.

Events create opportunities beyond the stage. Vendors, influencers, venues, artists, sponsors and media creators all have a part to play in Kenya’s entertainment economy.

04

One connected ecosystem.

From events and media to tours, stays, car hire, merchandise and community initiatives, HIT KENYA connects different ways to discover, create and participate.
