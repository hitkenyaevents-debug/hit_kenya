1. Who handles refunds?

For independent events, the organiser sets event-specific refund conditions, subject to Kenyan law and the disclosed purchase terms. HIT KENYA facilitates eligible refunds for transactions processed through its authorised payment system when available. For direct organiser payments, the organiser or payment recipient may need to issue the refund. If HIT KENYA is the supplier, it handles its own refund obligations. A WhatsApp enquiry is not a completed refund request until sent and received.

2. When a refund may be due

Refund eligibility may arise when an event is cancelled, a significant change affects the agreed service, a duplicate or incorrect charge occurs, the supplier fails to deliver, or the law or published event conditions provide a remedy. We will consider the circumstances and available evidence. Mandatory consumer rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 and other applicable laws take precedence over restrictions in event-level terms.

3. Cancelled events

If an event is cancelled without delivery, the organiser should notify attendees and arrange any refunds due. HIT KENYA will coordinate the process for purchases made through its enabled payment facilities. Eligible refunds should include the amounts required under law and the purchase terms. A voucher or credit may be offered as an option but will not replace a legally required monetary refund without valid agreement.

4. Postponed, rescheduled or materially changed events

Where the date, venue or other material element changes, organisers must explain whether tickets remain valid and how customers may seek a remedy. Attendees may choose to retain their tickets if acceptable. If the change creates a refund entitlement under applicable law or disclosed terms, the organiser and HIT KENYA will facilitate it. The notification should specify any applicable response deadline [EVENT CHANGE REQUEST WINDOW], without restricting statutory rights.

5. Situations where refunds may not be available

Subject to mandatory consumer protections and event-specific terms, a change of mind, failure to attend, arrival after disclosed entry cut-offs, or refusal of entry due to a lawful and clearly disclosed restriction may not qualify. Unauthorised resale, lost tickets or a used ticket are assessed on their facts. These exclusions do not apply where a supplier breach, unfair practice, statutory cancellation right or another legal entitlement requires a remedy.

6. Platform fees and service charges

Any booking, payment or platform fees must be disclosed before purchase. [CONFIRM FEE REFUND RULE]. Where law requires repayment of the full amount, fees cannot be retained simply because they are described as non-refundable. Any permitted deduction must be transparent, contractually applicable and lawful.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Contact HIT KENYA via hitkenyaevents@gmail.com or the WhatsApp contact below, or use the official organiser contact shown on your booking.
  2. Provide your booking reference, event name, purchase date, amount and a brief reason for the request.
  3. Provide only necessary supporting evidence. Never share a PIN, OTP, complete card number or identity document through an ordinary refund message.
  4. We will assess the request, contact the responsible organiser or payment provider as needed and communicate the outcome and next steps.

Request window: [REFUND REQUEST WINDOW]. This administrative window does not remove statutory cancellation or refund rights.

8. Processing times and payment methods

Once approved, refunds should generally return to the original payment method where possible. M-Pesa: [CONFIRMED M-PESA PROCESSING TIME]. Card payments: [CONFIRMED CARD PROCESSING TIME]. Other methods: [CONFIRMED PROCESSING TIME]. These are operational estimates to be confirmed with the payment providers; any mandatory legal deadlines take precedence. We will communicate material delays and the reason where available.

9. Ticket transfers and chargebacks

Tickets may be transferred only where the organiser permits it and the official transfer process is available. Do not share a ticket QR code publicly or sell tickets through unauthorised channels. If you believe a charge is incorrect or unauthorised, contact us and your payment provider promptly. You may exercise lawful chargeback or dispute rights; duplicate recoveries are not permitted. We will cooperate with valid investigations.

10. Complaints and contact

If a refund is refused or delayed, ask for an explanation and review. You may pursue appropriate remedies through relevant consumer authorities, payment providers or competent courts.
