1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small pieces of information saved by a website in your browser. Local storage is a related browser technology that can remember information on your device across visits. This policy covers both. HIT KENYA uses necessary storage for requested features and permits optional analytics only after you choose to enable it.

3. Your consent choices

Accept all permits optional categories; Reject non-essential leaves them off; Manage preferences allows individual choices. Optional preferences and marketing selections are recorded but do not activate any additional service in this release. The homepage's Google Analytics script is not requested until you permit analytics. Rejecting optional categories does not prevent browsing or using essential functionality.

4. Changing or withdrawing consent

Use Cookie preferences in the footer at any time. If analytics permission is withdrawn, the site disables future analytics collection, attempts to remove accessible analytics cookies and reloads the homepage if necessary. Withdrawal does not erase information already collected by a third party. You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser's privacy settings or block cookies entirely. Clearing necessary storage may remove saved listings, local reviews and consent choices.

5. Third-party services

Google Analytics is an optional homepage integration. Google Fonts, externally hosted images, hosting infrastructure, linked Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp services may process information according to their own policies when resources load or you follow links. The consent manager controls the identified analytics integration, not every third-party resource or independently operated destination. We will update this inventory if additional tracking or embedded media is introduced.
