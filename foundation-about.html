OUR FOUNDATION

People. Potential. Possibility.

HIT KENYA Foundation is presented as a community-focused initiative bringing together individuals, creatives, businesses and supporters around shared goals.

✦

OUR MISSION

To connect people with education, skills, creativity and opportunities that support stronger communities.

◉

OUR VISION

A Kenya where more young people can learn, participate, create and pursue meaningful opportunities.

✓

OUR VALUES

Integrity, inclusion, collaboration, accountability and sustainable community impact.

♧

OUR FOCUS

Education, youth empowerment, enterprise, creativity, wellbeing and environmental responsibility.

OUR APPROACH

REAL PEOPLE.
MEANINGFUL IMPACT.

We aim to build relationships that make programmes practical, inclusive and relevant. That means listening to communities, working with partners and measuring progress against clear goals.

Our project and participation figures will be published when they can be independently verified.
